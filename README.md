# What's all this about?

By day, I’m a Product Manager. By night, I’m usually found in my home lab, trying to figure out how to make a house "smart" - a good way to explore and test all the amazing tools/platforms out there be it those across local/Cloud, various coding agents, great IDEs etc.

Disclaimer: While I am consider myself a tad better than a beginner at system design, I am in no way a "no-LLM" coder. The projects here are the culmination of a rigorous debate with LLMs/Agents on design choices and coding practices and then the LLMs generating the code. 

Why does this repo exist? Most people see home automation as a hobby. I see it as the ultimate playground for testing the three things I care about professionally and leverage them for my personal hobby: economics, infrastructure, and scalability. If you’re looking for another profile full of "Hello World" tutorials, this isn't it. If yor hope this will give you some inspiration or direction to explore things yourself, this may be it. I promise to document my experience, trade-offs and the actual friction, if any, of building an intelligent (private-preferred) agent while attempting to get a sub-second LLM latency on consumer hardware or all the while figuring out the "Cloud Tax" of the system.

I hope something here sparks an idea for you. If you’re building something similar, or just want to argue about system architecture, let's talk.
